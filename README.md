# WorkManager

**One workspace for clients, projects, tasks, money and team, built for small companies and agencies.**

WorkManager replaces the mix of spreadsheets, messaging groups, separate invoicing tools and paper notes that small businesses use to run their work. It keeps every client, project, deadline, invoice, payment and expense in one place, and shows the owner a live picture of how the business is doing.

---

## The problem

Most small businesses and agencies run on a patchwork:

- Client details live in a contacts app, a spreadsheet, or a chat thread.
- Projects and deadlines are tracked in a notebook, a shared sheet, or someone's memory.
- Invoices are made in one tool, payments are tracked in another, and outstanding balances are checked by hand.
- Expenses are kept in receipts, bank statements, or a separate file.
- The owner has no quick answer to simple questions: *What is still unpaid? Which client is most profitable? What is due this week? Are we making money this month?*

The result is missed payments, duplicated work, lost files, unclear ownership of tasks, and hours spent gathering numbers that should be ready at a glance.

## What WorkManager does

WorkManager connects the parts of running a business that normally live in separate tools:

- **Clients.** Each client has one record and one readable code (for example `CL-0001`). Projects, tasks, payments, expenses and documents all link to that same client, so you choose a client once and it follows the work.
- **Projects and tasks.** Projects carry a client, status and deadlines. Tasks are assigned to team members, have due dates, and appear on a calendar.
- **Documents.** Quotations and invoices are created from the same client record, with automatic numbering (for example `INV-2026-0001`) that never repeats.
- **Payments.** Money received is recorded against the invoice it pays, so outstanding balances update on their own.
- **Expenses.** Spending is logged by category, with receipts attached.
- **Reports and dashboard.** Invoiced, collected, outstanding, expenses, net profit, open tasks and active projects are shown together, with trends over the last twelve months and breakdowns by client and by project.
- **Team.** Each new member is approved by an admin and sees only what their role allows.
- **Activity log and notifications.** Every important change is recorded with who made it, and reminders are sent for upcoming deadlines.
- **Manager assistant (optional).** An assistant that answers questions about the company's own data, when it is connected to an AI key.

## How it solves the problem

**One source of truth.** A client exists once. A payment is recorded once, against the document it belongs to. Every report and total is calculated from those same records, so the numbers agree with each other.

**Links instead of re-entry.** Start from a client's page and add a project, task, payment, expense or document with that client already filled in. You never re-select the same client on every form.

**Automatic, safe numbering.** Client codes and invoice numbers are generated by the database itself, so two people creating records at the same time cannot receive the same number, and the company's numbering continues from wherever it stopped.

**Clear ownership and control.** Admins approve people and set roles. The activity log shows who changed what, so disputes about a payment or a deadline can be settled from the record.

**Plain answers to business questions.** The dashboard answers the questions owners ask most often, without needing an accountant to produce a report first.

## Why it is better

| | Typical setup | WorkManager |
|---|---|---|
| Client details | Copied into several tools | Stored once, linked everywhere |
| Invoice numbers | Manually typed, sometimes repeated | Generated by the database, never repeated |
| Outstanding balance | Calculated by hand | Updates when a payment is recorded |
| Profit view | Needs an accountant or spreadsheet work | On the dashboard, by month and by client |
| Team access | Shared logins or open access | Each person approved and limited to their role |
| Record of changes | Often none | Activity log of who changed what |
| Data location | Spread across several vendors | One company, one database, owned by that company |

## How it is built

- **Interface:** a fast web application that also installs to a phone or desktop home screen and keeps the interface available when the connection drops.
- **Data:** stored in a PostgreSQL database with row-level security, so each person can only read and write their own company's data.
- **Sign-in:** Google sign-in, followed by a passcode for each session. Sessions expire after 12 hours, and the passcode locks briefly after repeated wrong attempts.
- **Ownership:** each company runs its own installation. The company's data is kept in its own database, not shared with other companies.

## Who it is for

- Digital, creative and marketing agencies that juggle many clients and deadlines
- Freelancers and consultancies with several clients running at once
- Service businesses with a small team that currently manage work in spreadsheets or messaging apps
- Owners who want to see profit, outstanding payments and expenses without a full accounting system

## What it is not

WorkManager is a workspace for running client work and tracking money in and out. It is not a full accounting or payroll system. It does not file taxes, and it does not replace a qualified accountant. Companies that need statutory accounts still need to export their figures to their accounting software.
